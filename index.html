About this guide

This guide covers the changes made to SaGa 2 – A Haniwa's Contingency, along with in-depth tips and tricks for beating the game: strategies for monsters and bosses, missable items, new abilities, equipment, and the new worlds.

It assumes some basic knowledge of the original game. Stat values are for Veteran and Insane difficulty unless noted; Average is often a little higher and Lunatic a little lower.
