IOverview

This guide covers changes made to Saga 2 - A Haniwa's Contingency, along with in-depth tips and tricks to beating the game. In particular, it focuses on strategies for monsters and bosses, missable items, new abilities, equipment, and the new worlds. This guide assumes you have some basic knowledge of the game and know where to go next. If you don’t, there are plenty of other guides online with this basic information. This guide instead focuses on changes made to the game along with advanced tips and tricks.

IICharacter/Class Changes

The following is a list of character/class changes that might affect who you pick.

Mutants

  • Mutants now learn magic by eating meat dropped by monsters. This enables them to learn new abilities quicker while also letting you decide if/when the Mutant will lose that 4th ability.
  • Besides the usual Fire/Ice/Thunder, Mutants have many new non-elemental abilities. These work on any monster regardless of their strengths/weaknesses. This makes Mutants much more useful against bosses compared to the original game.
  • Mutants are very effective at healing the party. Besides the usual “Cure” ability, Mutants can also learn “Heal”, which will cure the entire party instead of just one character at a time.
  • Mutants no longer gain elemental weaknesses/affinities. X-Ice, X-Fire, and X-Thunder are gone.
  • By using shields, Mutants can easily gain the defense stat. This means you could theoretically give them natural armor. It’s very hard to boost the strength of a Mutant, however. NOTE: There are optional patches to revert meat-learning as well as allow strength stat gains.

Monsters

  • This transforming class has been given a major overhaul in this hack. You no longer have to spend the whole game trying to transform into a Sprite/Fairy (although those are still great choices).
  • There are many new monsters you can transform into, and lots of previously useless classes are now useful. For example, try turning into a frog to discover a potentially powerful move early on. Also check out the Dragon and Wyrm classes; they are not a let-down like they once were! Each monster class now has something useful about it.
  • Having at least one monster in your party should offer a whole lot of fun, if not to at least see some of the new abilities and overworld graphics/sprites.
  • The new Larvae monster at the starting screen replaces the Baby-D and is basically the same. It is just a fun new sprite!

Humans

  • Although this class is expensive to maintain and still needs quite a bit of training, they are potentially the best at taking down bosses.
  • There are new weapons for humans that make them even more useful than before. Agility based weapons are still a great choice, but strength based weapons have been improved. You will be rewarded for purchasing those expensive swords and training the strength stat!
  • Magic books should not be dismissed for Humans! They can in fact be made into good mages if you train them well. Humans can actually gain Mana faster than Mutants.
  • Just like in the original game, Humans are NOT able to gain the defense stat very well.

Robots/Cyborgs

  • Robots are just as awesome as ever with their ability to recharge their weapons/inventory. They usually have the best defense and can be turned into walking terminators early on. They certainly make the first half of the game easier. However, Robots aren't always the best choice in certain battles/situations. They lack healing spells and magic use, and aren’t as good at using swords as Humans (at least early on).
  • There is a new class at the starting screen, which is a “Cyborg”. Cyborgs are the same as a Robot, but have permanent “Recover” instead of “O-Mortal”. This means the Cyborg has a "Recover" ability and will recover HP at the end of each turn. Some people might prefer O-Mortal for the fatal magic protection Robots get, but at the endgame Recover is more rare. Most endgame equipment blocks fatal magic, while recover is an ability that is near-impossible to obtain outside of the monster and mutant class.
  • Having one Robot/Cyborg will make your playthrough a bit easier in general. However, having too many Robots/Cyborgs will make the game much harder later since you will lack healing/magic. A choice of 4 Robots will make the game VERY difficult to beat! NOTE: The martial arts bug has been fixed with Robots/Cyborgs. This means you can no longer use up martial arts and retain the agility boost you get from it. This is a notorious bug in the original game that could make Robots extremely overpowered!

IIIWalkthrough

World 1Ki's World

#First outside Area

Monsters
  • Asigaru
  • Jaguar
  • Larvae
  • Gang
  • Skeleton
  • Goblin
  • The first monsters you encounter deal more damage compared to the original game, but have less defense to compensate (you may notice the Colt dealing a lot of damage here; this will not be the case beyond world 1). Mr. S will wipe the floor with these monsters, as he should. Just have Mr. S use Fire when in trouble.
  • You can have your characters gain a few stats while Mr. S is in the party if you desire – but it should be known that it’s difficult to gain stats against the low level monsters found in this world. Your chances are much better in the coming worlds when you face higher level monsters. If you insist on attempting to gain a few stats, have Mr. S use Cure to keep your party going while your Human(s)/Mutant(s) use swords, bows, and magic to boost their strength, agility, and mana. If you fail to gain any stats, don’t worry about it. You can easily beat this world by merely purchasing a few items and gear.
Monster Tip (level 1)
  • If you have the Monster class in your party, the question will be whether or not you want to eat meat in the first world. There are some better level 1 monsters than there were in the original game, but it’s also a pretty safe bet to keep your monster as-is until world 2. If you decide to eat meat, Fungus and Toad are not bad options since both have the Toxin ability that attacks/poisons a single group of monsters.
  • If you know what you are doing, you can change your monster into something slightly better than a mere level 1 monster. There are various monster transformation guides online (search for FFL2MonsterEvo for my favorite). Although these guides have different names compared to this hack, they give you a pretty good idea of what to do.
Mutant Tip (level 1)
  • Right off the bat, mutants have the chance to learn the following abilities/magic: Cure, Warning, Fire, Ice, and O-poison. You might consider keeping Cure on at least one mutant. You can pick up this ability at any point of the game, but it becomes much harder to learn later as your mutant gains access to more magic/abilities.
  • NOTE: Mutants can no longer gain elemental weaknesses like X-Ice, X-Thunder, and X-Fire.
Robot/Cyborg Tip
  • For most of the game, focus on defense and agility with robots/cyborgs, especially if you only have one. Strength is a mostly useless stat for them until you get endgame weapons (discussed later in the guide).
  • Enemies will attack the lead character the most, so if you have a robot/cyborg in the lead with high HP and defense, your party will hold up better. Load your robot up with armor and items to boost defense and HP.
  • Remember, robots/cyborgs can equip multiple of the same piece of equipment! This means they can have 2 helmets, 2 body armors, 3 gloves, etc…
  • Shields are a great choice for robots. Unlike other characters, robots gain a defensive stat from shields, plus they can use the shield to block attacks during tougher battles.
  • Agility is a great secondary stat for robots so they can carry out their turn first before any of the enemies do. You will have a major advantage in battle if your robot is extremely fast!

#Wyrmling

  • The Wyrmling is no joke, but with Mr. S, you should be able to win in 1-2 turns. Just use his Dissolve or Fire for a quick win. No strategy needed!
  • NOTE: Wyrmling can possibly drop level 2 Meat, which is not a bad idea to feed to your Monster or Mutant. Fore more information on level 2 monsters and magic, see the World 2 section!

#First Town

  • After the first cave, Mr. S will depart, leaving you more vulnerable to enemies. Going straight to town to purchase Bronze gear is recommended.
  • Give the character in the lead position a bronze shield to absorb most of the enemy attacks, since monsters will attack the lead party member the most. The robot/cyborg is a good choice for the lead character since you can easily boost their HP and defense by loading up their inventory.
  • For weapons, you can purchase a Long Sword, Rapier, or Punch (if you didn't start with one) in order to train your Human(s). If you have a robot/cyborg, consider giving them a Rapier or Bow for an agility boost. The more expensive weapons and gear can be skipped for now, such as the Gold Glove (unless you really have extra GP to blow).
  • NOTE: The Rapier causes more damage than Punch initially, but Punch will rapidly exceed it in terms of power after about 10 uses. It's really not a bad way to cause some decent damage. By the end of world 1, that punch should be dealing well over 100 damage while growing your Human(s) agility.
Human Tip
  • Unlike the original FFL2, agility based weapons are not always the best choice for Humans. Strength based weapons will begin to surpass the damage output of agility based weapons by about world 2. However, there are certain bosses/battles in which higher agility is necessary, so be sure to train both stats!

  • As long as your party has bronze gear and basic weapons, this should be a breeze.

If you have the patience, fight some extra flies, lizards, and slimes on the fourth floor. With some luck, you will be able to afford the incredibly useful “SMG” weapon for your Robot after the ruins in the coming town.
MAGI Tip
  • Don't forget to equip MAGI on your characters right away!
  • Nearly all MAGI can now be used in-battle as an item, weapon, or spell. You have to scroll all the way to the bottom of the inventory list in-battle to see it. For example, if you equip Defense MAGI, you can use it as a shield in-battle. Speed MAGI will give you an agility boost for that one battle. Fire/Ice/Thunder MAGI will grant the corresponding spell. See the MAGI document for more info on each!
  • For Humans, consider either the speed or power MAGI, depending on their weak point. For example, if your human is gaining more strength, give them the speed MAGI to boost agility. If they have gained more agility, give them the power MAGI to boost strength. You can also just focus on agility and ignore strength altogether, but later on this strategy won't allow your human to deal max damage with the more powerful strength based swords coming up.
  • For Mutants, the mana MAGI makes the most sense, unless you have them doing something other than using magic…
  • For Robots, consider the defense Magi – especially if they are the lead character. This will make them even more indestructible. Another option is the speed MAGI so they strike first (especially once you obtain an SMG).
  • For monsters, the best MAGI depends on their category. For example, if you have a monster that needs more defense, give it the defense MAGI. If you want it to attack first, give it Speed MAGI.
  • NOTE: Despite rumors you may have heard, MAGI will NOT effect how fast your characters gain stats. It is true that stat-boosting gear will reduce the chances of gaining stats at the end of a battle, but MAGI has no such downside.

#Second Town

  • Most expensive items in this town can be ignored for now. The exception is the SMG, which you should purchase the moment you have enough GP. Once you do, give the SMG to your Robot/Cyborg. If you don't have a Robot, give the SMG to a Human or/or Mutant and use it sparingly (only target large groups of monsters).
Robot/Cyborg Tip
  • Try combining the SMG with gear that grants higher agility (Bows, Rapiers, Punch, etc). Your robot/cyborg will be able to wipe out a whole group of monsters with the SMG before the monsters even get a chance to attack!

#Assassin

  • The new mini-boss at the end of Ashura's base is an "Assassin". Don’t take this guy lightly! The Assassin will kill one character each turn without fail unless you block the attacks.
  • It's possible to win by just attacking and losing a few characters (as long as Ki stays alive), but the safer strategy is to have the lead party member use a bronze shield every turn. RARE DROP: If Assassin drops an item, there is about a 50% chance it will be a Kimono. Even more rare (less than 15%) is a Katana. The rest of the time will be a Long sword.
  • The Kimono armor cannot be obtained for a little while and offers a nice agility boost. The Katana is also nice should you be lucky enough to get one, and is the most powerful sword for 2 more worlds to come.

Monsters

  • Scorpion

  • Snake

  • WereRat
  • Wyrmling rare, 1/32 chance
  • Beware of the Troopers and Pebbles here. When you first arrive, they will deal some major damage. Have your lead party member use a shield to block these attacks.
  • If playing on Lunatic or Insane skill, Zombies can use Curse. Be sure to purchase a Curse potion or two.
  • Pick up the Silver gear in the item shop as soon as possible. The Silver Armor is available right away, although it might be too expensive initially.
  • If you have extra GP, consider purchasing a Kick at the weapon shop. The Kick will deal decent damage after about 10-15 uses.
  • NOTE: If you happen to get either your Kick or Punch down to just one single use, save it for the upcoming boss. It will pack a huge punch (no pun intended).
Robot/Cyborg Tip
  • As mentioned earlier in the guide, robots/cyborgs can equip as many gloves/helmets/armors as you wish. However, unlike the original game, they do actually benefit from higher end gear!
  • With the exception of the Bronze series, robots will always gain more defense from higher priced gear. For example, the Silver Helmet grants a robot 2 more defense points than the Silver Glove, while the Silver Armor grants 4 more. This means you will sometimes want to purchase that more expensive body armor instead of a bunch of cheap gloves. You could even equip your Robot with more than one piece of body armor for maximum defense, but this would get very expensive...
Monster Tip (level 2)
  • World 2 has better monster transformations than world 1, but that is if you haven't already transformed into a better monster using some clever meat eating. Some good monsters include the following:
  • Pebble, which is great for defense. If you pick a defensive monster like Pebble, it’s a good idea to place them in the lead position if their defense is higher than other characters (like the robot).
  • Red Bone has good attack power.
  • Wyrmling has a lot of HP and good attack power.
  • Scorpion has a new ability called "SndStorm" (Sandstorm). SndStorm attacks all monsters and ignores their elemental properties. It is similar to Acid, although it does not cause poison and cannot be blocked via poison immunity.
  • Nautilus is another good choice with the Acid ability.
  • TIP: If you kept the starting monster Larvae until now, you may eat the meat of a Were-Rat to turn into a Medusa. You can then eat the meat of an Eagle to turn into an Obake, then the meat of a Snake to turn into a Sprite. This path is slightly different than the original game, but gets you the best monster for at least the next 3 worlds…
Mutant Tip (level 2)
  • Now that you are fighting level 2 monsters, your Mutant(s) can learn level 2 magic such as Thunder, O-Death, and O-Fire (you can also continue to learn level 1 magic as well). Thunder is arguably the best elemental magic attack, since very few monsters are immune to it. It is definitely worth giving up one of the four magic/ability slots to retain thunder.

#Second Desert Town

  • As of version 2.0, the desert world is slightly different. To navigate it, merely follow the cactuses to a path leading through a cactus forest on the far south end of the map.
  • Now is a good time to stock up on some cure potions to get through Ashura’s Tower. You may also consider some eyedrops, since some of the monsters coming up can blind you.
  • The Battle sword and Sabre can be purchased, but you don't really need to unless you have more than one human. You will be getting one of each for free in the Tower coming up. If you have a human or mage with high agility as well as mana, consider a Psi Sword.

#Woodman

  • The Woodman fight can be a challenge if you have a weak party. He has a lot of HP and can deal some decent damage. Have your lead character use a shield if you must. Woodman is weak to Fire, so have one of your characters use Fire/Flame if you have it.
  • NOTE: Woodman can drop level 3 meat, which has a chance to teach your Mutant a level 3 ability or upgrade your monster to level 3. See the section in the next world for more info on level 3 monsters and Mutant abilities.

#Mask

  • Please note that Mask is NOT automatically equipped with the axe like he is in the original game. Instead, you will obtain the axe and must equip it through the menu. Or, you can always keep the axe and give the Mask something else. If Ashura is too hard, consider giving the Mask a Battle Sword. This will cause more damage than the Axe each turn. Just know that once you equip it on the Mask, you can't get it back.

#Ashura

  • The fight against Ashura can involve a little luck. If Ashura decides to use Flame repeatedly, you probably won’t survive. On the other hand, this battle can be easy if Ashura decides to keep attacking your lead character and you have a shield. This is the time to use your SMG, Battle sword, and Punches/Kicks (if you used up enough of them).
  • Your Mutant(s) should stick to healing, but if you feel you must attack, have them use a PSI sword if you bought any. The Mask will be the one who wins this battle for you (mostly).
  • If you have trouble winning, consider giving the Mask the Battle Sword instead of the Axe for more damage. If you just so happen to have a monster in your party with Stone, this will instantly kill Ashura.
  • Ashura has about 1,700 HP on insane skill, with about half that on Average skill and somewhere in the middle with veteran skill.
  • DROP: There is a 20% chance Ashura will drop one of the following items: Axe, Giant Glove, Geta, Power Potion.

#Town

  • There is a lot to buy here, but the problem will be having the gold to afford it all. You should probably skip purchasing the Giant's Armor and Helmet due to how outrageously expensive they are at this point of the game (return later if you manage to get a boatload of gold). Instead, here are some alternative purchasing options:
  • Gold Bow for your Robot(s), which has a nice agility boost and deals decent damage.
  • Katana for your Human(s), which is a nice step up from the Battle sword (more so than in the original FFL2).
  • Geta Boots for your Human(s) and/or Mutant(s), for the strength boost and extra help with defense (you will also get another pair in Giant's town for free).
  • Giant's Glove for your Human(s) if you can afford it, for the awesome strength and defense boost. If you aren't even close to affording a Giant's Glove, consider purchasing the less expensive Gold Glove if you are lacking in defense.
Armor/Gear Tip
  • You should keep in mind that equipping stat-boosting gear will slightly reduce the chances of a character gaining that stat at the end of a battle. The higher the stat is, the lower the chances are of gaining that stat. Since giants gear boosts strength a lot, it will lower the chances of gaining a Strength stat at the end of a battle. That said, it’s still probably worth the sacrifice to get the benefits of the giants gear on a Human using strength based weapons. Later on, if you wish to max out stats, you can just remove stat-boosting gear.
Monster Tip (level 3)
  • There are a few good monsters to transform into at this point. The Worm has Sandstorm similar to the Scorpion, and both the Ammonite and Crab have Acid. Woodman and Triceras are also good choices for their large amount of HP and high attack power. Fiend comes with the "Fire" ability, which is ok. Oni has Ice, which the upcoming boss is weak to. If you are lacking in the defense department, Cobble or Woodman are decent choices. The best monster to have against the upcoming boss is Red Bone, since it can use defense as well as reverse the effects of "Absorb" with its undead status. More info on that in the upcoming boss section.
Mutant Tip (level 3)
  • Mutants can now learn StonSkin, O-Ice, and Steal in this world. StonSkin can have its uses if you know which monster it will work on. That said, you can probably pass up on retaining these level 3 abilities unless your Mutant is lacking other, more useful abilities like Cure, Thunder, Fire, Ice, etc..
Robot/Cyborg Tip
  • If your Robot has started to decline in the agility department, give them a Gold Bow or two. That should allow them to strike first and wipe out a group of foe with the SMG before they get a move. The Gold Bow is also good vs. monsters with high HP.

  • This battle could take a while depending on your party setup. Just like Ashura, Venus is immune to elemental magic - so don't bother with Fire/Ice/Thunder. Venus can use Charm, PSI Whip, Flame, and Fatal.
  • For starters, you should have a lead character with high defense that can use a Flame Shield (you might consider purchasing one if you don't already have it). This will block Venus's Flame magic and PSI Whip. All you have to worry about now is Charm and Fatal. Thankfully, Venus seldom uses the Fatal magic. Charm is the biggest threat here and is used frequently.
  • The most useful character in this battle is likely to be a human. If you give your human character an agility based weapon, Speed MAGI, and use the TrueEye MAGI, you should be able to deal 500+ damage to Venus each turn. This tactic could technically also work for a Mutant or Robot.
  • You can also use a Strength based weapon with Power MAGI if your human has high enough agility to land a hit on Venus (and high enough strength to deal damage). This would be more powerful, but the trick is actually hitting Venus.
  • For monsters, Biochem or Scorch work against Venus (see the section above for monster tips). If you have a monster with a powerful attack like Centros with 3-horns, consider using power MAGI to boost strength even further.
  • For Mutants and/or Human mages, consider having them heal, or if confident enough, use a PSI Staff or the Scorch ability.
  • If all else fails and your characters are under-trained, consider purchasing a few Magnums and have your lead character use a Flame shield every turn while everyone else fires upon Venus.
  • Drop: Venus has an 80% chance of dropping one of the following: PSI Whip, Elixir, Remedy, Magic Potion. The PSI Whip and Remedy may be purchased later, but not for a few worlds.
  • NOTE: Those familiar with the original FFL2 may notice Venus no longer gives the Aegis MAGI. This was an intentional change for balancing purposes (basically, the Aegis shield was too powerful and made most of the upcoming equipment redundant). Don't worry though; the Aegis is not gone for good!

#Apollo

  • This battle is unchanged from the original FFL2, which means it is still the toughest boss fight up until this point! Apollo will chat for the first five rounds rather than attack, using his Aegis shield each turn. Once Apollo transforms, he will begin to use Flare and Masamune every time. You will pretty much be dead in just a few turns unless you use Heal Staffs. Apollo has about 25,000 HP, but will start imploding/melting once he is below 10,000 HP or 20 turns has gone by - whichever comes first. There are two main methods to defeating Apollo:

#Method #1

  • This method is usually easier, and merely involves staying alive until Apollo starts imploding/melting, which is 20 turns (including the first 5 turns before he has transformed).
  • For this method, just give everyone a Heal staff who can use one. For the first 5 turns, you can do anything you want, including attempting to "Run" (it won't work, but it will pass the time faster).
  • Starting on the 6th turn after Apollo transforms, have everyone use a Heal staff. You will need to have enough Heal staffs to last the next 15 turns. If you are still alive after those 15 turns,
  • Apollo will begin to implode/melt. One turn later, you will win the battle as long as one character is alive besides Dad!

#Method #2

  • If you think your party is strong enough, you can attempt to kill Apollo the “manly” way by either getting him below 10,000 HP so he implodes, or by getting him all the way to 0HP so he dies.
  • For this method to work, you must use your powerful weapons AS SOON as the battle begins. Keep in mind that since Apollo uses the Aegis Shield, you will need to use defense piercing moves to get through his defense. The most powerful Attacks that will work include the Glass Sword, NukeBomb, Gungnir, Excalibur, Beam Gun, Flare, PsiShot, PSI Gun, PsiBlast, Tornado, Whirl, Sun Sword, and Cyclone magic. Other attacks that will work, but with lower damage include Scorch, Tide, Tank, Vulcan, and other guns.
  • For the first 5 turns, have all of your characters attack. Don't bother Healing until Apollo transforms! Once Apollo transforms, have at least two characters use Heal staffs. Consider using Dad to Heal along with a Mutant. Your other characters should continue attacking. With any luck, Apollo will be below 10,000 HP before he decimates your party. Once he starts melting, you have to survive just one more turn!
  • Remember that you must have at least one character alive at the end of this battle other than Dad to win.

  • Zirok has more HP than any other boss in the normal game: 40,000 HP on insane skill, 25,000 on veteran skill, and 15,000 on average skill. The good news is that Zirok lacks O-Weapon, so you can deal more damage than you normally would against previous bosses like Magnate, WarMach, and Odin. The trick to this fight is being able to survive long enough to kill Zirok.
  • Zirok has 6 abilities total. His most common is 2-Bites, which can inflict a lot of physical damage. Your lead character should have high defense and O-Weapon, or use the Ancile Shield at the start of the battle. Do NOT have your lead character use a shield every turn, since it will be necessary to have two characters healing while the others attack to chip away at Zirok's large amount of HP. Zirok can also use Comet, Flare, and PsiShot.
  • If you have two characters Healing every turn, you should be able to survive Flare and Comet. PsiStaff is the most deadly since it can kill a single character in one hit, but thankfully it is more rare than other attacks. Zirok can also use Calamity and Curse, both of which you should be immune to if you use the Ancile Shield or even the Aegis Shield at the start of the battle. Anyone not using a Heal staff should be attacking Zirok.
  • If you find your healers to be too slow, try using Isis’s TrueEye to boost their Agility.
  • If you have a Robot/Cyborg, use Gungnir, or at least a Samurai Bow. Have a Human use Excalibur, and have your monster use whatever their powerful move is. It might be wise to choose a healing monster for this battle, but another option is a monster with “Accel” or “Buff”. If you spend multiple turns buffing your monster, they will start dealing massive damage against Zirok. See the level 11 monster guide for a list of monsters that have accel and buff!
  • Your Mutant will likely be Healing. If not, PsiShot is a decent attack against Zirok. Isis should use Masmune every turn. If you lose more than half of your team, have Isis use "Heart", which will bring them all back to life. You should wait as long as possible to do this, since it is your only lifeline!
  • It might take 15-20 turns, but with some patience, Zirok will fall.

#Notable Dangerous foe and tips

  • Arachne, SlimeGod, Reptilian: By this point you probably already faced Reptilian, but SlimeGod and Arachne are new in this area. The next danger is Arachne, since it has a small chance of using Tornado. Arachne should be targeted after SlimeGod. Reptilian is not a massive threat, although be careful to avoid attacking them with physical attacks in case they counter with a Revenge Blade (this will deal massive damage).

For Musashi, consider using Fatal magic against him since he has no immunity to it (there are more strategies above with Musashi).
  • Black Belt, Paladin, Susanowo: You may have encountered Black Belt and Paladin already (see above for tips), but just be aware that their new friend, Susanowo, has a chance to use Tornado. You may consider taking him out first before dealing with the other two!

Oceanus will be the biggest threat of the 3, but you may consider taking out the other two first so you aren't ganged up on. Oceanus has a lot of HP, so it will likely take a few turns to defeat the group. The hardest part of this battle is the incredibly high defense of both Genbu and Dagon. High impact magic like Flare, PsiBlast, or PsiShot are the way to go.
  • Revenant and Wizard: You probably already know the Wizard by now (see above for more info), but the Revenant will be new. It might still be best to take out the Wizard first, but Revenent can also pose a threat. Consider having your lead character use a good shield in this battle to block touch.

#Haniwa

  • Ah, the legendary monster itself (it's even in the hack name)! The Haniwa is by far the toughest regular encounter; it is even debatable if it's tougher than all the bosses you can fight. Haniwa has between 10,000 HP and 12,000 HP depending on the difficulty you chose.This may not seem too bad compared to most bosses, but the issue is how Haniwa recovers 10% of it’s HP every turn. This means if you aren't dealing more than 1k-2k damage per turn, you should just run away (which is possible, unlike with the rest of the monsters in the final area). Now, any fan of the original game will remember being able to end this battle quickly by just using a Hyper Gun. This time, it will NOT work. You will need to fight Haniwa the old-fashioned way.
  • Haniwa has 4 abilities: Bash, Quake, Flare, and the Seven Sword. The first two moves on that list can be easily blocked/avoided with good armor or shield. The latter two are where the danger lurks. Flare can nearly wipe out your entire party in just one hit. Most characters will have about 10% of their HP left if they survive. Robots/Cyborgs may not survive at all since they don't have any Mana (Mana reduces the damage delt by Flare and other magic). If Flare wasn't bad enough, the Seven Sword is almost worse. The chances of a character surviving an attack with Seven Sword is naught unless they are using a shield. The tough part is being able to afford having a character use a shield every turn instead of attacking when Haniwa recovers 1,200 HP. For this reason, you might have to just take your chances and not use a shield at all, except at the start of the battle to block Quake the remainder of the battle. You should also have a character or two using a Heal Staff every time Haniwa casts Flare, which will further prolong the battle. The best moves, as with most bosses, are defense piercing or never-miss. This includes Gungnir, Excalibur, Beam Gun, Flare, PsiShot, PSI Gun, PsiBlast, Tornado, Whirl, Sun Sword, Cyclone magic,Glass Sword, NukeBomb, Samurai Bow, etc...
  • For monsters, the best abilities are Touch, Beam, Inferno, Comet, Gandiva, Bident, Scorch, BioChem, and Tide. If you have a monster with Buff or Accel, you will definitely want to spend 3+ turns boosting the agility/strength of that monster, then proceed to attack. This should dish out massive damage. You just have to hope Haniwa doesn’t use Seven sword on them while they buff/accel.
  • Isis should use Masmune most of the battle, which will cause about the same damage as Flare. She should also save that Heart to bring your characters back to life when things get bad.
  • In the end, the battle against Haniwa might involve some luck that he doesn't use Flare and/or Seven Sword regularly, but you CAN typically win with a strong party after about 10-20 turns. There is about a 1/8 chance Haniwa will drop the famed Seven Sword when you win this fight (which is a higher chance compared to the original game).

Spoilers, Seven Sword Drop:

Veterans of the original FFL2 will know the rumors about obtaining this incredibly rare sword. Even more lucky yet are those who actually managed to get one! The good news is that Haniwa has a much higher rate of dropping one than before at 1/8 chance. In the original FFL2, it was closer to a 1/64 chance. The sword itself comes with 7 uses and can hit up to 7 times. The Seven Sword has a better chance of working when given to a character with high strength and agility. If either stat is low, it won't perform as spectacularly as it could. With high strength and agility, it will not be uncommon to see this sword dealing anywhere from 2,000 to 5,000+ damage. Considering how rare this sword is, you could give it to a Robot/Cyborg to gain 3 permanent, rechargeable uses. The only drawback is the complete lack of stat boosts you gain with the sword, so it will eat up a slot with no benefits (beyond how powerful the sword is). Alternatively, you could give it to a Human and choose to use it very sparingly! Consider saving it for the last boss, or for the post-game (or use it against another Haniwa).

Other Drops: It should be noted that as of version 2.0, Haniwa can also drop Body/Speed/Magic/Power potions. There is also a 1/16 chance of it dropping a Life Potion, Gungnir, Arthur Armor, or Parasuit!

#Preparing for the final boss

  • This is that part of the game when you must decide if you want to beat the final boss right away, or if you want to max out your characters, get some rare drops, and possibly even the famed legendary sword. However, there is a reason you might not want to linger too long here, which is mentioned in a spoiler box in a few pages.
  • There are many ways to prepare for the final boss, but rather than list it all in one big paragraph, here are tips for each character class:
Monster Preparation Tip
  • If you want to play the final battle safe, consider picking a monster that can heal your entire party. It's an even bigger bonus if the monster you choose has high agility, like Chiron with 95 agility. This is because a monster with high agility will get to heal your party before the final boss attacks each turn. Other good choices for healing include Gargoyle with 94 agility, and Madam with 94 Agility. Earth is a bit slower but has higher defense.
  • Treant is a fantastic choice too, since it has the Life ability along with Heal and can bring your party members back. The only downside to Treant is lower agility at 78.
  • Alternatively, you may choose a monster that deals damage against the final boss, like Titania (PsiStaff), Ghost (Touch or Flare), Rhyos (Comet),Tianlong (Inferno), Lich (Flare), Athtolot (Flare), Beholder (Beam or Comet).
  • Similar to the strategy mentioned for Haniwa, you can always pick a monster with buff or accel and have the monster boost its stats for 3+ turns before attacking for mass damage. Examples include Tianlong, Fenrir, Rhyos, Kusanagi, Kraken, T-Rexaur, Susanowo, and Cerberus
  • See the “Monster Guide” document for a list of all possible monster choices!
Mutant Preparation Tip
  • It's a good idea to switch your Mutant(s) back and forth between healing and attacking during the final boss battle. The best attacks by far are PsiShot or the PSI Gun. If you don’t have either, use Flare (buy a Flare book if you have to).
  • Don't forget to equip at least one Heal staff on the Mutant. Two is recommended.
  • Also consider placing your Mutant in the last position and give them the Life Potion from the treasure chest if you haven’t used it. Should the Mutant survive an onslaught, they can bring everyone back to life at that critical moment. Keep in mind you do NOT have Isis to help you in the final battle, so you won't get the use of the Heart MAGI.
Human Tip
  • It can be tricky deciding what to have your Human do. You might be tempted to use Excalibur, but it will be dealing less than 500 damage unless you have over 99 strength (with gear). As such, you might be better off equipping a Human with Beam Guns, since they pierce the high defense of the final boss. If you saved up Glass Swords, now is the time to equip and use them.
  • If your Human has high Mana, you can also give them a PSI Gun, which will definitely be superior to a Beam Gun. If you lack a healer and your Human has high Mana, give them a Heal staff.
  • Your Human is probably the best choice for the lead character to take hits. Load them up with all your best armor (see the “King Arthur” tip earlier for some good choices), and equip an Ancile shield if you have one.
Robot/Cyborg Tip
  • Your Robot/Cyborg should probably be your main source of damage against the final boss.
  • If your Robot is low in the strength department and is dealing less than 500 damage each turn, then equip your Robot with a Beam Gun. This will deal about 500-550 damage consistently every turn.
  • If you followed the "Gungnir robot god" tips above in this guide, you can easily create a Robot that deals 700-900 damage against the final boss with the Gungnir spear.
  • Your Robot/Cyborg just needs at least three top swords, such as x2 Gungir and x1 Sun Sword, or x1 Gungnir and x2 Sun Swords, or x3 Gungnirs (if you got the rare Odin Drop).

SPOILER: In the original game, the fun ended once you defeated the final boss. In fact, with maxed out characters, you could beat the final boss in one anti-climactic turn! However, this time around you should consider going straight to the final boss when ready. This is because afterward, you have the option to continue playing with a post-game quest! You can then continue beefing up your characters and maybe even go for that Seven Sword if you desire (not required of course). Should you proceed with the post-game quest, you will face brand new bosses that require the strongest characters possible.

#Arsenal

  • Here it is, the final battle (kind of!) This battle will be significantly different on insane difficulty compared to Veteran and Average. Arsenal has only slightly more HP when compared to the original game (about 11,000 vs 10,000). However, Arsenal comes with Recover, an ability that recovers 10% of its health every turn. This means Arsenal will recover about 1,100 HP every turn - similar to a Haniwa. On Veteran and Average difficulty levels, Arsenal is not all that much different from the original, minus some new abilities it may use.
  • At the start of the battle, you should use the Ancile shield to lessen the damage of certain attacks carried out by Arsenal in this battle. Then, you must destroy the 4 laser cannons before you can attack Arsenal itself. They have about 1,000 HP each. Target-all attack spells like Flare are useless at this point since you can only attack one laser cannon at a time. Heal only if you get low on HP, and don't delay destroying the Cannons or they will repair/revive.
  • Once the 4 cannons are gone, Arsenal will enter its second phase. You can now damage Arsenal in this phase. In the original FFL2, the only attack Arsenal could use in the second phase was "Smasher". This time around, it can also use a Beam Gun, NukeBomb, and ComVirus (although Smasher is the most common). The Smasher Attack can also deal far more damage than it once did. In some cases, it can exceed 800 (much less on average difficulty).
  • Make sure your characters are healed between turns. You might not actually need to use a Heal Staff in this second phase unless Arsenal uses a NukeBomb. Instead, you can use a normal Cure spell on whichever character got hit with Smasher/Beam. If you are unlucky enough to have Arsenal use ComVirus on your Robot/Cyborg, it will probably kill them in one hit. You can always use a Life Potion if this happens, if you have one.
  • Arsenal will begin recovering about 1,100 HP per turn at this point (insane difficulty only), so you might need to have 3 out of 4 of your characters attacking and one healing. See the previous section (preparing for the final boss) for some good attacks to use.
  • The third and final phase begins when Arsenal has about half of its HP left. It will then launch its main Smasher attack every single turn, which will deal anywhere from 20 damage to 750 damage, (a very wide range). You can choose to use 2 Heal staffs every turn at this point if you want, but it may be difficult to deal more than 1,100 damage per turn by doing so. Instead, just observe how much damage Arsenal causes each time with Smasher. If you happen to get a good turn and Arsenal deals less damage, then switch one of the Healers to attacking.
  • Eventually, you should chip away at Arsenal's HP and win the battle.

Congratulations, you have beat the normal game! Now, read on to the next page if you desire another quest…

#Noteworthy Items to Purchase

  • Life Potion: These are just about mandatory in order to survive some of the boss fights coming up. However, before you purchase any, keep in mind there is also a way to get 3 free Life potions every time you use them up (see the world 1 beast section coming up). If you need more than 3 Life potions at any time, then consider purchasing additional potions at the store.
  • Hero Helmet: This Helmet is quite simply the best way to boost your stats. Mana, Strength, and Agility all go up 15 points on a Human or Mutant. The only downside to this helmet is the lack of immunities/protections. For this reason, the Robot/Cyborg will not really benefit much, since it will not gain any stat boosts either. This Helmet pairs up well with the next item discussed.
  • Angelic Boots: These boots are potentially the best of all the endgame equipment. Although they don't offer much more defense than standard boots, they grant ALL immunities minus Fire and Poison. This means immunity to Quake, Stone, Fatal magic, thunder, and Ice. It also grants O-Weapon, so you can finally ditch those Ninja Gloves (and replace it with a Paladin Glove if you got one from the Paladin monster). If that wasn't enough, the Angelic boots also boost Mana and Agility 15 points, so you can also ditch those Hecate boots and Hermes shoes.
  • Falcon Sword: This sword is a good boss killer on a well trained character. It has the potential to deal more damage on a single target than any other sword minus the Seven Sword. The catch is you need high strength and agility for it to work well. The Falcon Sword can hit the target two times, but if you have low agility, the sword might only hit or miss altogether. This sword will work well with Humans or Robots/Cyborgs (or Mutants if you trained them to use weapons). It will also boost the agility of Robots/Cyborgs by 32 points, which is the ONLY strength-based weapon to do so. If you still have that a Karate taking up space in your Robot’s inventory, consider replacing it with this. The downside to this sword is that it can be blocked by shields, and the damage output is reduced by O-Weapon.
  • Railgun: Clearly this weapon must be good with a price tag of 550,000 GP on insane difficulty? Well, yes, it is. The Railgun is basically a Beam Gun on steroids! It will deal fixed damage from 1,100-1,600 damage on a single target, and the damage is completely unaffected by defense or O-Weapon. It can be given to anyone, although the Robot/Cyborg is a good choice due to the rechargeable 10 uses you get along with an agility boost of 28 points (only slightly lower than Karate or Falcon Sword). This weapon is a boss killer to say the least. The downside is the sheer amount of GP it takes to purchase!

#What to purchase first

  • Before going crazy and spending all your initial GP on a Railgun, instead consider purchasing a few cheaper items. For example, the Falcon Sword and Angelic Boots will initially be a lot more useful. Give a Falcon sword to your Robot/Cyborg and/or a Human. Save this sword for the upcoming boss fights. If you have a Human, purchase the Angelic Boots. You can now happily ditch those Ninja Gloves for your Human and replace it with the Paladin Glove (if you got one from a Paladin). Combined with Arthur Armor, your Human will now have extremely high strength, agility, and O-All.
  • As mentioned already, you don’t need to purchase too many Life Potions since you can get 3 at a time for free coming up, but if you have just a lot of GP to blow, then consider purchasing more than 3.
  • Later on you can grab a Hero Helmet and ditch the Dragon Helmet, but consider saving this as one of the final purchases you make.
  • With a Falcon Sword and Life Potions, you should be able to defeat most of the upcoming bosses. If you have a Mutant, the Parasuit is probably still the best choice.
  • When you get more GP later, it’s not be a bad idea to grab another pair of Angelic Boots for your Robot/Cyborg to make them immune to all (although having them use the Ancile shield might also suffice). You will need to purchase a Railgun later, but that is discussed further down the post-game guide.
Dad/Captain Equipment Tip
  • If you have the GP, the best equipment to purchase for Dad would be the following (once again, take your time):
  • Angelic Boots: this will make him immune to everything except poison and fire, will grant O-Weapon, and will boost his Agility and Strength.
  • Hero Helmet: This will boost his stats and defense even further.
  • Paladin Glove: This is not available from the store, but is a rare drop from the Paladin monsters. If you can manage to get one, it will boost Dad's strength even more, making his Captain Sword better (or any other sword you equip on him for that matter). If you don't want to farm a Paladin Glove, consider a Ninja Glove instead.

You might consider always leaving a Heal staff on Dad, and/or a Life Potion. The final slot should be a weapon to help you bring down bosses, like a Falcon Sword or a Railgun if you REALLY don't mind farming that much GP.

#Back to World 1 (Ki's World)

#Guha

  • This first beast can take a while since Guha has a lot of HP (10k – 30k depending on the difficulty chosen). However, your team should be dealing high damage by this point.
  • Guha will spend most of its turns using Absorb. If you aren't prepared, this will sap HP from you and give it back to Guha almost every turn. Although you can still win by allowing this, it will involve a lot of healing and take much longer. A better tactic is to have your lead character use a shield/tank/defend sword each turn to block absorb, or better yet, transform your monster into something that can reverse the effects of absorb.
  • A little bit of info about Absorb: Guha will NOT absorb HP when used against plant, rock, sword, or golem families of monsters, but it will still deal damage to them. Against Undead monsters, Absorb will actually take HP from the caster and give it to the monster! For this reason, if you have a Monster in your party, consider transforming them into an undead monster and put them in the lead. This will make Guha hurt itself instead of absorbing HP from you. If you lack a Monster, then consider having your lead character use a shield, as mentioned above.
  • Any magic will work on Guha minus fatal magic and stone, so feel free to use whatever you want. Just go with your most powerful attacks, which you should have down to an art by this point!
  • Tip: if you get yourself a chainsaw, you can kill Guha in a single hit if the character wielding it has over 105 strength. Any character with high strength + stat boosting gear could pull this off. Also the monster Lich can kill Guha in one hit with the Scythe. Kusanagi can sometimes kill it in one hit with Critical as well.
MAGI obtained
  • Defeating Guha will grant you with 1 of each primary type of MAGI. That's right, MAGI is back in your possession! Good thing too, since you will need it. Go ahead and equip MAGI on your characters as you did in the first half of the game.
  • Consider giving your lead character Defense MAGI, while other characters get the MAGI that will boost their individual abilities (Power for a Human using Excalibur, Mana for a Mutant using spells, Speed for anyone using agility based weapons, etc...)
  • You may also consider equipping Speed MAGI on a Mutant or Monster that will be healing your party so they can heal first before a monster/boss attacks. Sometimes this will give you the edge you need to win a battle.
  • You will notice that Guha also grants the Pegasus MAGI. Now you don't have to purchase anymore Doors!

#Back to World 2 (Desert World)

  • The Second beast may be found in the desert of the second world. The easiest way to find it is to simply stand to the left of the town/village, and travel west until you hit the desert. As soon as you hit the desert, travel south until you reach a clearing. The next beast is in that clearing.

#Jorgandr

  • This beast has more HP than just about any other boss (up to 60,000 on insane difficulty), although you can deal a lot of damage since it lacks O-Weapon. The main issue with this beast is not the amount of HP it has, but rather the amount of physical power it has. If you take no action to block attacks, Jorgandr can deal 1,000 - 2,000 damage per hit on a character using one of its two physical attacks (2-Bites or 4-Heads). Basically, you will lose a character every time it hits unless you block the attacks.
  • The Ancile shield is your best bet here on the lead character. If you lack one, the Defend Sword might suffice, although it only blocks attacks 70% of the time while the Ancile has 100% block rate. A Tank might work also, but is pretty weak at this point and only has a 60% chance of blocking attacks.
  • Besides physical attacks, Jorgandr can also use SndStorm, Sleep, and PsiBlast. PsiBlast can just about wipe out your entire party if they aren't healed all the way, but the good news is that Jorgandr seldom uses it. SndStorm can also hurt due to Jorgandr's high Mana, but nothing you can't handle (about 400-500 damage per character).
  • By using a shield, Defend Sword, or some other move with your lead character to block attacks, the rest of the battle just involves patience. Have your Mutant or Dad (or both) use a Heal staff whenever Jorgandr uses SndStorm and PsiBlast, and use a Life Potion whenever Jorgandr kills one or more characters. Consider giving the Life Potion to your Mutant in the last position, or even to Dad (if you made him better with gear and/or potions). This battle is long, but very doable!
MAGI obtained
  • Along with one of each type of common MAGI, you will also obtain the TrueEye. You may once again use this MAGI to boost speed/agility during boss battles if you need it, although likely by this point you don't with all that stat-boosting gear!

#Back to World 4 (Apollos World)

  • The third beast may be found south of the port town. Go into the sea and travel south until you hit the wall at the end, then travel east. You should see the beast wandering around in this area.

#Neptune

  • As it turns out, those villagers were correct. Neptune does exist! This beast is a step up in difficulty from the other two beasts. Neptune has less HP than the previous beasts (about 28k HP on insane difficulty, 18k on Veteran, and 9k on average), but he has far more deadly attacks.
  • Neptune can use the incredibly powerful PsiStaff. This will either kill a character in one hit, or leave them with very little HP left. You could use a heal staff every turn, but this will prolong the battle and allow Neptune to eventually use Whirl on you, or even worse, a Trident. The Trident will deal 800-900 damage to every character. If you get unlucky enough to have Neptune use Trident twice in a row, it will likely be game over.
  • A good option for this battle is to place a character in the lead with an Ancile shield, and have them use it every turn. The Ancile shield will repel PsiStaff and Tide. Your most powerful attackers should focus on killing Neptune promptly.
  • Try to only use healing when when necessary (when Neptune uses Whirl or Trident), and instead focus on killing Neptune as fast as possible. Alternatively, you can just use a Life Potion to give everyone their full HP and not heal much at all (Life potions will heal others in addition to bringing them back to life)
  • Another option would be to have your Mutant(s) attack every turn with a Rune Axe, which will repel PsiStaff and Tide.
  • If you have a Monster with the Accel or Buff ability, use this for 3+ turns and start dealing mass damage with attacks.
  • If you are struggling to win this, purchase more Life potions back at the Central Temple and have two characters with them (possibly a Mutant and Dad). This will lessen the chance of Neptune killing the only character that can bring others back to life.
  • TIP: If you have the monster Lich, it can kill Neptune in one hit with the Scythe. Kusanagi can sometimes kill it in one hit with Critical as well. Neptune can technically be killed by a chainsaw, but you would have to have over 160 strength, or about 140 + lots of Power MAGI. The only character that can achieve this is a Robot/Cyborg.

SPOILER: You may be shocked to see that Neptune drops meat (100% drop rate). Eating this meat will turn your monster into a secret hidden monster: Neptune himself! This is a very useful monster transformation for the upcoming battles. Neptune comes with PsiStaff, Trident, Whirl, Tide, Cure, Life Potion, O-All, and Surprise.

WARNING: if you eat ANY OTHER MEAT after transforming into Neptune, you will transform back into a level 11 monster and lose Neptune! There is actually one more chance later to get Neptune back, but this would be a huge loss unless you prefer a different level 11 monster for some reason…

#The SeerSuit

  • Remember the wizard NPC back in the central temple “Café”? Return to him since you have defeated 3 beasts. He will give you a “SeerSuit”, which is a better Parasuit. In addition to boosting every single stat, it will grant 88 defense points and O-All. Perhaps the most unique trait of this suit is that is also comes with “Recover”. You can’t really go wrong with giving this suit to any character, but of course a Human or Mutant will benefit the most from the stat boosts. However, a Human would likely still have higher strength and agility from other gear stacked up. Likewise, a Mutant could have higher mana from other gear stacked up. A Robot isn’t a bad choice, since it lacks recover. A Cyborg would be an ok choice as well, although it already has built-in recover. If all else fails, you can give it to Captain!

#Back to World 6 (The Lost City)

  • To find the next beast, you have to navigate back through the marsh south of the traveler’s town (see the Lost City instructions earlier in the guide). Enter the old temple on the Northwest end of the map. Once inside the old temple building, go around the center room to the back part of it (don’t enter the building where the Yojimbo mini-boss was). There you will see a throne with a monster sitting on it. This is the next beast.

#Ballor

  • This fight will initially seem very difficult. Ballor has between 25,000 and 55,000 HP depending on the difficulty you chose, and some very deadly moves.
  • The most common attack Ballor will use is Rune Axe. You must be aware that even though Rune Axe is his weakest attack, it will repel any magic back to you! Absolutely do NOT use PsiStaff, PsiShot, or any other spell that can be reflected back. Flare will work fine, or if you have a Neptune monster, use the Trident.
  • The next move Ballor will often use is Scythe. Unfortunately, even if you have immunity to fatal magic, the Scythe could instantly kill a character.
  • The other trouble with this battle is Ballor’s tendency to use FleshRot. If you have a Human or Mutant as the lead character, this will decimate them.
  • The solution to this battle is to place a Robot/Cyborg in the lead position, and have them use a shield or defend sword to block the Scythe and Rune Axe. A Monster might also work, but you might not have a good defense move with your current monster (Neptune doesn’t have defense).
  • The last attacks you have to worry about are Tornado and Inferno, but thankfully those are seldom used. Just be prepared to bring characters back with Life Potions, and use your most powerful attacks.
MAGI obtained
  • Defeating Ballor will grant you the Aegis MAGI. It's possible you can think of a use for it, but the Ancile Shield is always superior at this point of the game.

#Back to World 7 (Venus World)

  • To find the next Beast, exit Venus Town and travel to the west. When you are almost at the cliffs, you will see a monster slightly above you in the trees.

#Deity

  • This beast is sort of a combination of Jorgandr with physical attacks and Neptune with magic attacks. The most common attack used here is 6-arms, which will deal very high damage if your character is low on defense, lacks o-weapon, and/or isn't using a shield.
  • Deity will also often “Buff” itself, which will make 6-arms even more deadly.
  • The other common ability is PsiBlast, which will target all party members. Deity can also use PsiShot, which will target a single character (mana-based).
  • To at least block PsiShot and 6-Arms, have your lead character use an Ancile shield every turn. The only thing you truly have to worry about now is PsiBlast, which will attack all characters and require a Heal staff or Life Potion to recover. Deity has up to 30,000 HP (depending on the difficulty), which isn't as bad as some other beasts. Deity has O-All, so you will need to use those defense piercing moves and weapons. RARE DROP: There is a 1/8 chance that Deity will drop the Angelic shoes. Even though you can purchase these, it's a very nice drop since it spares you from having to spend the gold!
MAGI obtained
  • Defeating Deity will grant you the Heart MAGI. This is potentially even more useful than it was the first time you had it, since you can give it to a Monster that otherwise cannot equip Life potions.

#Back to World 9 (Edo)

  • To find this beast, travel east until you get to Hanna's village. Once in front of the village, travel North across the bridge. Now travel around the backside of Hanna's village. To the Southeast of Hanna's village is the next beast.

#Goryo

  • This humanoid Beast has the least amount of HP at 17k – 23k, but can wipe out your party fast if you don't defend. Dullahan's Falcon sword can deal 1,500+ damage to everyone if they aren't using a shield, even with O-Weapon!
  • Avoid using normal swords and physical attacks against Goryo, or he will sometimes counter attack (which will deal insane amounts of damage). Instead, rely on magic attacks and weapons that don’t allow a counter attack (Gungnir and Excalibur qualify).
  • Similar to the other bosses, the strategy is to have your lead character use an Ancile shield and/or Defend sword each turn. Once you do that, this battle should be over pretty quick when you attack with your most powerful moves.
  • If you have the monster Lich, it can kill the Goryo in one hit with the Scythe, Kusanagi can sometimes kill it in one hit with Critical as well. Goryo can technically be killed by a chainsaw, but you would have to have over 160 strength, or about 140 + lots of Power MAGI. The only character that can achieve this is a Robot/Cyborg.
MAGI obtained
  • Defeating Goryo will grant you the Masmune MAGI. Sadly, the Masmune isn’t as powerful as your other attacks at this point, so you likely won’t be using it.

#Back to World 11 (Valhalla)

  • The final Beast is south of Valhalla Palace (the east exit). When you teleport to Valhalla Palace, you will already be standing on the east side of the palace. Travel straight south, and you will notice a Fairy standing in front of a bridge (the fairy isn’t the boss, but you may talk to her). You will also notice a bridge connecting these clouds to the rest of the area that wasn't previously there. Cross the bridge to face the beast!

#War Mk.2

  • This will likely be the toughest of the 7 Beasts. War Mk.2 has 40k HP on insane difficulty, 30k on Veteran, and 15k on average.
  • The main problem is the primary weapon of War Mk. 2: The Railgun. This will deal 1,100+ every time War Mk.2 uses it, and there is no way to block it. As such, you should expect to lose a character nearly every turn. Other attacks that can be used by War Mk.2 include Beam, Biochem, Nukebomb, and ComVirus. If you get lucky, War Mk.2 will use these moves on you more than Railgun.
  • The strategy is to have at LEAST two characters with Life Potions, and another character with the Heart MAGI. Place those characters in the back of the party so they aren't attacked as frequently. Don't even bother healing in this battle, unless War Mk.2 uses NukeBomb. The Railgun will one-shot every character anyway, so your goal will be to just have everyone attack. When you lose about 2-3 characters, have one of your characters use a Life Potion to bring them all back. Repeat this process until you have won!
  • If you are struggling to deal enough damage, consider purchasing a Railgun of your own back at the Central Temple.

#Preparing for King Haniwa

  • Now it's time to return to the Central Temple (don't forget you can take the elevator straight down to the final area without going through the whole temple). This is a great time to do some stat gaining, GP collecting, and item gathering to be successful with the Next boss fight.
  • King Haniwa is a huge step above the 7 Beasts, and you will need all the best items to win. The Seven Sword would help you win this upcoming fight, but is not required. Regardless, it can’t hurt to hunt Haniwas for that 1/8 chance they will drop a Seven sword, since you will also be getting lots of GP in the process of trying to farm one. Don’t stress out if you don’t get the sword…
  • If you don’t already have one, get enough gold to purchase a Railgun. If you don’t manage to land yourself a Seven Sword, consider purchasing a second Railgun. King Haniwa will be much easier to defeat with x2 Railguns or x1 Railgun and x1 Seven Sword. Unfortunately, the cheaper Falcon Sword will not be very useful against King Haniwa due to his ability to block it.
  • Don’t forget to stock up on lots of Life potions! Consider having 3 characters with Life potions - or at least two of them with Life potions and one with the Heart MAGI.
  • Consider placing your Monster as the last, or second to last position in the party. The reason for this is explained in the spoiler below.

#King Haniwa

  • This battle will involve a lot of strategy to win. King Haniwa has 22,000 HP on insane difficulty, 17,500 on Veteran, and 13,000 on average. This means that King Haniwa will recover approximately 2,200 HP every single turn on insane skill.
  • The primary attack of King Haniwa is a new sword called "Asi", which cannot be dodged, and will kill a character in a single hit. The Asi will also block physical attacks as well as reflect magical attacks back to you. For this reason, you do NOT want to use PsiShot, PsiStaff, or any other reflective spell against King Haniwa.
  • The Seven Sword is the second most used attack by King Haniwa (more frequently than a normal Haniwa). Other deadly attacks include Flare and Comet. If you get lucky, King Haniwa will waste a turn with X-Bash (O-Weapon should allow your characters to survive X-Bash).
  • The best method to win involves equipping two characters in the the back of the party with Railguns and/or a Seven Sword. These two characters (possibly a Robot/Cyborg and Human) will need to be attacking every single turn. If you fail to do over 2,200 damage per turn, King Haniwa will recover his HP. Whenever possible, have a 3rd character attack as well to deal even more damage. The following attacks cannot be blocked by King Haniwa: Railgun, Excalibur, Gungnir, Flare, Trident, PsiBlast, Comet, Whirl, Tornado, and Beam gun. The Seven Sword CAN be blocked by King Haniwa's Asi Sword, but it causes enough damage to be worth using even if it fails half of the time (you will be unlucky if King Haniwa blocks you more than that).
  • The other party members who are not attacking, including Dad, should be using Life Potions when necessary. Consider giving Dad 9 Life potions (which takes up 3 slots). Since Dad will be attacked the least, he should be your lifeline against King Haniwa to revive fallen party members. You can attempt to use your first two characters to attack King Haniwa, but more than anything they will be cannon fodder against King Haniwa's Asi and Seven Sword. You can always have your lead character use an Ancile shield every turn, which will block the Seven Sword and X-Bash (but NOT the Asi). With a little luck, they will survive 2-3 turns before falling.
  • You may find it easier to ignore Healing staffs altogether in this battle, and instead have everyone attack. When two or more characters die, have someone use a Life Potion, which will bring all party members back to life as well as Heal those who are alive. Using this strategy will also allow you to defeat King Haniwa faster.

MAJOR SPOILER: Upon defeating King Haniwa, you may or may not be shocked to see him drop meat! Hopefully you have one monster left alive at the end of this battle (consider repeating the battle if not, which you are allowed to do). The reward for eating this meat is transforming your monster into a mighty Haniwa! This transformation is a no-brainer, and should be chosen even over Neptune. You will lose those Life Potions, the Trident, and PsiStaff with Neptune - but you will gain Flare, Comet, Heal, and the ultimate attack: the Seven Sword! The best part of Haniwa's Seven Sword is having all 7 uses that are fully rechargeable!

King Haniwa's reward
  • After defeating King Haniwa, he will hand over a lot of MAGI and explain that he was not the mastermind behind stealing the MAGI. In fact, he was being controlled by the "Dark Lord" along with the rest of the Haniwa. As such, he will request that you free the Haniwa and in turn gives you his ultimate weapon: the Asi sword.

#Asi Sword

  • The Asi sword is a single group-attack weapon with unlimited uses like Excalibur, but is far more powerful. It will also block all physical attacks as well as reflect magical attacks. Basically, the Asi is a combination of Excalibur, Defend Sword, and Mirror. Give this to a Human in place of Excalibur. If you have a second Human, give one Excalibur and the other the Asi. If you have no other Human, consider giving the Excalibur to a Robot/Cyborg or Dad. Although Dad's Captain Sword is slightly more powerful against single monsters, Excalibur is better against groups of monsters and cannot be blocked.

#Preparing to face Dark Lord

  • King Haniwa mentions traveling back to your hometown for the path to be revealed. Before doing so, you should make sure to restock supplies that you likely used up. Purchase lots of Life Potions, and don't forget to use a Tent or visit an Inn.

#Locating Dark Lord

  • Now it is time to go back to your hometown. Warp to the first town, and walk back through the very first cave. Once you arrive on the other side, enter your hometown, go to the school on the north side, and speak to Mr. S; he will tell you about a passage that is now open on Mystic Mountain (speaking to him will also open this passage). Note: The parents of the 4 main characters do say some new fun stuff (including Mom).
  • Now you can either walk to the first village again, or simply use a Door/Pegasus to warp there. Travel north of the first town. Remember that giant mountain? It now has a cave to enter.

#Mystic Mountain

  • This cave is only one area, but it’s a bit long. You must wrap around a spiral to the very center. It’s pretty hard to get lost, since there is only one way to go (and only one possible dead-end if you turn right instead of left at the start).
  • The only monster you will encounter here is Haniwa. Thankfully, the encounter rate is very, very low. You will likely only encounter 1-2 Haniwa’s the entire walk, if you encounter any at all. You may also simply run from them. Note: Now is a good time to hunt for that Seven sword if you never got one.

#The 7 Beasts revived

  • At the very end of the spiral, you will see a ghost. He will reveal some shocking information, and proceed to punish you for being disobedient. Before you may face the Dark Lord, you must defeat all 7 Beasts again. The catch is you must defeat them all in a row!
  • The 7 Beasts are completely unchanged and require the same strategy as before. However, this time you have an important weapon that will help immensely: the Asi Sword. Give your lead character (possibly a Human) the Asi sword and use it every turn against the 7 Beasts. This will effectively block/repel most attacks from the 7 Beasts while also dealing some good damage.
  • The rest of the characters should change their roles depending on the beast you face, but you should give your characters in the back of the party those Life Potions (especially Dad). Try to use them sparingly, when you feel you are in trouble.
  • Most of the Beasts should be pretty easy with the advanced weapons you now have. If you transformed a monster in your party to the Haniwa, it would be wise to save the Seven Sword for the final battle against War Mk.2.
  • At the end of this entire ordeal, your resources may be dwindling, and War Mk.2 is the hardest of the bunch with the Railgun. The Seven Sword will help you immensely in winning this final battle quickly.

#Preparation (for the last time)

  • Now it's time to prepare for the final battle in the post-game. After you have defeated the 7 Beasts, use a Tent, and make sure you have plenty of Life Potions still. You really should have at least one Seven Sword going into this final battle (your Haniwa monster with a Seven Sword will suffice).

#DarkLord

  • The final boss has about 63,000 HP on insane skill, 50,000 on Veteran, and 30,000 on average. DarkLord will also recover 10% of its health every single turn, which is 6,300 in insane difficulty. This may sound impossible, but DarkLord actually has fairly low defense, and lacks O-Weapon.
  • The primary attacks used by DarkLord are Scythe, Rune Axe, Critical, and Inferno. There is a very, very slim chance DarkLord will use Flare, which can kill every single character if you are unlucky enough - even if they have 999 HP.
  • Have your lead character attack with the Asi sword every turn to block the Scythe and Rune Axe attacks. Otherwise, the Scythe can kill a character every turn. Rune Axe is not as deadly, but can reflect those PSI moves from your Mutant back to them, which will likely kill them. At this point, you mostly have to watch out for Inferno and Critical. Critical will kill a character without fail. Inferno will kill anyone not at full HP, including Dad. Any time you lose a character, use a Life Potion to bring them back, otherwise you won't be able to deal enough damage to kill DarkLord.
  • Just remember that you need to be dealing over 6,550 damage every turn. Since DarkLord has low defense and lacks O-Weapon, this is very possible. You should be dealing a lot of damage with those powerful swords. In fact, the Asi Sword equipped on a character with Power MAGI + Paladin Glove + Arthur Armor + Hero Helmet should be dealing well over 3,000 damage every time. The easiest way to pull off a victory is with the Seven Sword + Asi + Some other powerful attacks (Dad's Captain Sword, Excalibur, Gungnir, PsiStaff, Railgun, etc.)
  • Although the Railgun will deal some decent damage, a fully beefed up robot/cyborg can probably deal 2,000+ damage with the Gungnir spear, while a Railgun can only deal up to 1,500 damage. If you aren't dealing this much damage, consider sacrificing agility/defense on the robot and instead load them up with strength based weapons/items (Excalibur, Sun Swords, Modules to name a few).
  • The Seven Sword can deal anywhere from 4,000-7,000 damage. With some good strategy (and some luck), you will be dealing 11,000-13,000 damage total on the average turn, except when you lose some characters and need to bring them back to life.
  • Unless you keep getting unlucky, you should win this battle in about 12-15 turns. The tricky part will be winning this battle after you have drained all 7 uses of the Seven Sword. Unless you happen to have two Seven Swords, this will mean that ALL your characters should be attacking, except the one character using a Life Potion should someone fall. This battle will likely take a handful of tries to pull off, but it is doable!

#Return to Isis

  • After you have defeated DarkLord, you will get some new dialog from King Haniwa, Byakko, and Isis. You will also notice there are no more monsters (perhaps DarkLord was controlling all these monsters the whole time?)
  • Talking to Isis will give you the option to go back home, thus cueing the credits and ending the post-game content.
  • Hope you had fun! :)
