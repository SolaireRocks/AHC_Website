Agility-based Martial Arts

  • Martial arts get more powerful as the number of uses reduces, plus whatever the multiplier value is listed here (minus the defense of the monster). There is a slight bonus in damage if the characters AGL is higher, but in general AGL doesn't effect the damage as much with Martial Arts as it does with agility-based weapons. Robots/Cyborgs will deal little to no damage with Martial arts (even with high agility), but they will get a nice AGL stat boost by equipping them. If you have a fast Robot/Cyborg, consider agility-based weapons instead for dealing damage.

Mana-Based Magic/Books

  • Magic books will of course deal damage based on the Mana of the character using them. Unlike strength and agility-based weapons, magic/books will ignore the defense stat of the target and instead be affected by the Mana stat. If the target has higher mana, the magic will deal less damage. There are a few spells that ignore the mana defense of the target, however (mentioned in the special notes).
  • NOTE: If using insane difficulty, some magic books will deal about 1 point less in damage.
