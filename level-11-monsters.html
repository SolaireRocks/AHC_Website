Level 11 Monsters

Stats, abilities and attack / defense / support ratings for every level 11 monster.

  • Since there are a lot of good level 11 monster choices in this hack, I have decided to create a separate document for more detailed information about these monsters.
  • Each monster has been given a rating in three main categories: attack, support, and defense. Some monsters are decent in all three categories, while others accel in just one or two categories. This list helps you know which monster is good for what.
  • NOTE: the monsters you actually fight in the Central Temple do not have these exact same stats. Some of them actually have higher HP since the characters are capped at 999HP, and often they cannot use all the abilities you can. However, they are pretty close…
  • NOTE #2: These stats are for Veteran and Insane difficulties. On average difficulty, some of these monsters will have lower HP (which is a catch 22; your monster character has less HP, but the monsters you fight also have less HP. This is made up for in other ways, such as better equipment stats).
