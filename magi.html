MAGI

What every MAGI does when equipped, and what it does when used in battle.

On this page

  1. MAGI List
  2. Default MAGI List (optional patch ONLY)
  • The following is a list of all MAGI available in the game, and what they do when equipped.
  • If a MAGI has an in-battle effect, you must look at the VERY bottom of the ability list of that character while in a battle to use it. When used, it will grant the effect listed.
  • Most MAGI have limited uses in-battle, but can be recharged via Inn, Tent, or Elixir.
  • Keep in mind that the “in-battle” effect is stacked ON TOP of the stat boost and immunity when used. For example, the Power MAGI will boost the strength of the person equipped with it by default, but using it in-battle will stack another 10 points of strength on top of the normal boost (just for the battle it is used in). MAGI may boost/train stats upon winning a battle as well.
  • Note: If you reverted MAGI back to the default behavior via the optional patch, refer to the second list further down.
